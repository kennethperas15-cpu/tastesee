Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Taste & See, Cebu City, Philippines

1. What we collect

  • Google account basics (name, email address, profile photo) when you choose Sign in with Google.
  • Order details you provide: name, phone number, pickup or delivery address, and ordered items.
  • Community photos and captions you voluntarily share to Taste & See Moments.
  • Rider location, shared only by signed-in riders while an assigned delivery is active.
  • Checkout safety data: approximate device metadata (browser user-agent, screen and platform details), an approximate location derived from your delivery address or IP address, and your IP address. We use these only to enforce the daily order limit and to block duplicate orders from the same location and phone/device unit.

2. How we use it

To take and fulfil your orders, show order status and rider location, run the store (analytics, stock, staffing), enforce ordering limits and prevent duplicate or fraudulent orders, and display community photos you choose to share. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Sharing

Delivery riders see the name, address, phone number, and items of orders assigned to them. Staff see orders needed for baking, delivery, and administration. We use Google for sign-in and Supabase for secure data storage; both process data under their own policies. No online payments are collected on this site.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in our Supabase project with row-level security so customers only see their own orders, riders only their assigned deliveries, and staff only what their role needs. Despite these safeguards, no internet service is perfectly secure.

5. Your choices

You may sign out at any time, delete your own community photos, and ask us to correct or delete your personal data by contacting us through the store. Deleting staff or order records needed for business records may be limited by law.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: message us through the Taste & See storefront or email the store owner. We will update this page when our practices change.
